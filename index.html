Global SCI-Fest 2.0

"The science of tomorrow, today" — Organized by GSS Science & Innovation Club

About the Event

Global SCI-Fest 2.0 is a one-day national-level science celebration featuring Olympiad exams, treasure hunts, exhibitions, and interactive experiences. The event is designed for students of grades 9–12 to test academic excellence and logical problem-solving skills.

Program Outline

  • Date: Mangsir 26 (Friday)
  • Venue: Global School of Science Premises
  • Start Time: 8:00 AM sharp
  • Activities: Olympiad Exam, Treasure Hunt, Science Stalls, Food Stalls, Student Creations Showcase
  • Audience: Schools and colleges from across the country, free entry for visitors
